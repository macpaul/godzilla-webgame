# 怪獸設定研究 — Kaiju research notes

Source research for the ten-level campaign. Every stat, attack name and
weakness below is taken from the cited article, not invented, so the game's
dossier cards and move sets stay faithful to the films.

Research date: 2026-10-08.

## Sources

| Source | Used for |

Roster shortlist drawn from the Wikizilla list, Showa → Heisei order:
Anguirus, Ebirah, Kamacuras, Rodan, Mothra, Gigan, Hedorah, King Ghidorah,
Biollante, SpaceGodzilla, Mechagodzilla, Destoroyah, Megalon, Titanosaurus,
Orga, Monster X / Keizer Ghidorah, Battra, Fire Rodan, Kumonga, Manda,
Gorosaurus, Baragon, Varan, King Kong.

Ten were selected on three criteria: **name recognition**, **mechanical
variety** (each must fight differently), and **a rising difficulty curve**.
Dropped and why:

- Kamacuras / Kumonga — same "swarm of insect limbs" role as Ebirah.
- Mechagodzilla — a beam-mirror mirror-match; SpaceGodzilla already covers
  "reflects your beam", and Destoroyah is the better finale.
- Megalon / Titanosaurus / Orga / Monster X — weaker recognition than the ten
  kept; no unique mechanic to add.
- Mothra larva, Fire Rodan, Hedorah's saucer stage, Biollante's second form
  and Destoroyah's aggregate form are kept **inside** their boss fights as
  phase changes rather than spending separate levels on them.

## Player: ゴジラ Godzilla

| | |
|---|---|
| Species | 怪獣 King of the Monsters, prehistoric reptile irradiated by H-bomb tests |
| Height / weight | 80 m / 60,000 t (Heisei) |
| Element | 放射 radiation |

Canonical kit, from the [Godzilla (Heisei) abilities section](https://wikizilla.org/wiki/Godzilla_(Heisei)):

| Move | Canonical basis | In game |
|---|---|---|
| 放射熱線 Atomic Breath | blue-orange beam from the mouth, "roughly equal in power to Ghidorah's gravity beams" | main ranged attack, costs radiation energy |
| レッド熱線 Red Heat Ray | reddens when overheated or enraged | atomic breath tier 2 upgrade |
| 放射火炎 Fire Ray | Burning Godzilla's orange-red flame beam | super, unlocked late campaign |
| 全身放射熱線 / Nuclear Pulse | energy vented from the whole body, a defensive burst | AoE guard-breaker, unlocked mid campaign |
| 尾獣払い tail sweep, 踏みつけ stomping, body slam, claw swipe | "Physical abilities": tail, stomp, grapple, bite | punch / kick / stomp / tail |
| 水中 amphibiousness, high intelligence, durability | "Amphibiousness", "Intelligence", "Durability" | guard, i-frames on dash |

Weakness carried into the design: Godzilla's own radiation is a slow
self-destroying furnace — at the finale he is Burning and overheating, which
justifies the campaign's final-form unlock and its time pressure.

## Level 1 — アンギラス Anguirus

| | |
|---|---|
| Debut | *Godzilla Raids Again* (1955), Showa |
| Title | 暴竜 Fierce Dragon |
| Species | giant ankylosaurus (armadillo / hedgehog / crocodile mix) |
| Height / weight | 60 m / (Showa) — quadrupedal, can rear up |
| Element | 地 earth / 獣 beast |

Attacks (canonical):

- **突進 charge** — "tends to attack with his claws and by charging opponents."
- **噛付き bite** — "rows of extremely sharp teeth … added bite force … cause pain to any kaiju."
- **尻尾回し tail swing** — "may use his spiked tail to swing around at opponents."
- **音波咆哮 ultrasonic roar** — in *Godzilla Raids Again* his roars "caused Osaka Castle to crumble"; ranged, and "unable to engage in beam locks with other monsters' energy weapons" → in game it is a cone wave that **cannot** clash with the atomic breath.
- **装甲 carapace** — "carapace is studded with a plethora of long, sharp, prickly spikes" → beam-resistant armour.
- **掘進 burrowing** — later Showa/appearances; used as a reposition, not a hit.

Silhouette brief: stout quadruped, crocodile-long snout with a short horn,
inward-curled head horns, two tusks, domed spiky carapace, tail longer than
the body (most of his length), hind legs longer than forelegs.

Game read: **armoured brawler**. Resists radiation (0.6×), takes normal melee.
Teaches the player that the beam is not a universal answer.

## Level 2 — エビラ Ebirah

| | |
|---|---|
| Debut | *Ebirah, Horror of the Deep* (1966), Showa |
| Title | 大エビ怪獣 Giant Decapod Monster |
| Species | giant mutant crustacean |
| Height / weight | 50 m tall, 60 m long / 23,000 t; pincers 15 m (right) and 13 m (left) |
| Element | 水 water |

Attacks (canonical):

- **右鋏 crush** — "the larger one on his right arm … used to grasp objects and bludgeon enemies."
- **左鋏 stab** — "his left claw, which is smaller and thinner, is used to stab and skewer."
- **触角whip / 脚 sweep** — "used his tail to sweep Godzilla off of his feet" (Tail Strike).
- **水鉄砲 pressurised water blast** — pressurised mouth blast strong enough to shatter a boulder (game-canon).
- **跳ね上がり leap** — "could leap high into the air out of the water."

Weakness (canonical): "Ebirah possessed an aversion to a yellow fruit … jets of liquid made from the fruit to ward him off"; and "while able to hold his own against Godzilla beneath the waves, Ebirah was outmatched" on land → **soft shell: radiation 1.4×, and he fights worse away from the shallows.**

Silhouette brief: red lobster body, one oversized pincer and one slim one, two
antennae curving back, forward whiskers, long down-curved scythe rostrum,
stalked pale eyes, brow ridges, two rows of red-to-white back spikes, six
walking legs, fan-tipped tail.

Game read: **zoner**. Long pincer reach and a projectile; punishes players who
walk straight in. Beats the beam but not the pincers at close range.

## Level 3 — ラドン Rodan

| | |
|---|---|
| Debut | *Rodan* (1956); in the Godzilla series from *Ghidorah, the Three-Headed Monster* |
| Title | 空の大怪獣 Giant Monster of the Sky |
| Species | giant irradiated pteranodon |
| Height / wingspan | 50 m (Showa) / 70 m (Heisei); wingspan 120 m |
| Element | 風 air |

Attacks (canonical):

- **音波衝撃 shockwave** — "capable of creating destructive shockwaves when flying, which he has used to demolish major cities."
- **羽ばたき wind gusts** — "powerful gusts of wind … knock other kaiju down" → knockback, no damage.
- **急降下ダイブ dive / talons and beak** — "uses his beak and talons effectively … his beak was powerful enough to crush boulders and disable Mechagodzilla's right eye."
- **掴み lift** — "can lift creatures several times his weight, for instance, Godzilla" → grab-and-drop.
- **ウラニウム熱線 Uranium Heat Beam** — as Fire Rodan, "a radioactive heat beam from his mouth" → phase-2 upgrade mid-fight.
- **supersonic flight** — "outmaneuver and outrun supersonic jets."

Silhouette brief: upright pterosaur, brown/burgundy skin, spiky chest crest,
two to three horns swept back from the head, broad membranous wings.

Game read: **aerial**. Hovers out of melee range, dives in, leaves a shockwave.
Beam is strong against him in the air (1.5×) because he cannot guard while
flying; melee only lands on the dives.

## Level 4 — モスラ Mothra

| | |
|---|---|
| Debut | *Mothra* (1961); vs Godzilla from *Mothra vs. Godzilla* (1964) |
| Title | 巨蛾 Giant Moth / 怪獣の女王 Queen of the Monsters |
| Species | giant divine moth (egg → larva → cocoon → imago) |
| Element | 風 air / 鱗粉 scales |

Attacks (canonical):

- **鱗粉 scales** — "yellow poisonous powder that can incapacitate enemies"; later "given the ability to reflect attacks back at her enemies" → DoT cloud **and** a beam-reflect shield.
- **糸 silk** — "spit a stream of silk at her enemies to either disorient or entrap them" → bind.
- **風 gust** — "flap her wings to create gales which tear apart buildings and knock other kaiju down."
- **触覚ビーム antenna beams** — "can fire laser beams from her antennae" (Beam Pulser).
- **尖刺 stingers** — "a hail of poisonous dart-like stingers from her abdomen"; a single larger stinger that can "immobilize enemies pierced by it."
- **死と再生** — "an imago's death to coincide with its larvae hatching" → **phase change: the imago falls and the larva rises**, a second, slower but heavier form.

Silhouette brief: cream-and-red furry thorax, huge pale-green/cream wings with
brown eyespots, stubby head with large dark eyes, curled proboscis; larval form
is a fat brown segmented caterpillar with glowing blue eyes.

Game read: **controller**. Slows, binds and poisons rather than hitting hard.
Teaches that damage-over-time can kill you while you play safe. Melee through
the scale cloud is the answer; beams get reflected.

## Level 5 — ガイガン Gigan

| | |
|---|---|
| Debut | *Godzilla vs. Gigan* (1972), Showa |
| Title | サイボーグ怪獣 Cyborg Monster / 未来怪獣 Future Monster |
| Species | cybernetic space dinosaur |
| Height | 65 m (Showa) / 120 m (Millennium) |
| Element | 機械 mech |

Attacks (canonical):

- **回転カッター buzzsaw** — "a Rotating Cutter … built into his abdominal area … considerable durability and sharpness" → sustained multi-hit DoT, his signature.
- **ハンマーハンド hammer hands** — "large metal hooks … uses his Hammer Hands to batter and stab opponents, drawing blood from Godzilla."
- **レーザーアイ laser eye** — "a beam aperture … set into his forehead just above his visor."
- **ブレードスライサー bladed slicers** — "guided razor discs … able to boomerang back for a second attempt if they initially missed" → returning projectile.
- **原子推進翼 flight** — "capable of flying at speeds of up to Mach 3" → dash flights.
- **背びれ sails / 金属の嘴 metal beak** — three fin-like sails, avian crest.

Silhouette brief: dark-green cyborg with golden scale plates, blue stripes on
thighs and shoulders, red cybernetic visor for an eye, metal beak, crest of
metal spines, circular saw on the abdomen, hook hands, spiked tail, three back
sails.

Game read: **armoured rushdown**. Metal plating cuts melee (0.7×) but the
electronics hate radiation (1.3×). The buzzsaw is a channelled attack — you
must interrupt it, not guard through it.

## Level 6 — ヘドラ Hedorah

| | |
|---|---|
| Debut | *Godzilla vs. Hedorah* (1971), Showa |
| Title | 公害怪獣 Smog Monster |
| Species | alien pollution creature; colony of Hedrium micro-organisms |
| Height / weight | 30 m landing stage, 60 m perfect / 48,000 t; 40 m flying saucer |
| Forms | Initial, Aquatic, Landing, Flying (saucer), Perfect |
| Element | 毒 toxic |

Attacks (canonical):

- **酸性スラッジ acidic sludge** — "composed almost entirely of … Hedrium, an acidic, sludge-like material … can launch pieces of its body at opponents, which will burn or smother them", fired with "impressive level of precision" → arcing blobs that leave burning pools.
- **硫酸ミスト sulfuric acid mist** — "the byproduct of Hedorah consuming" pollutants → area denial fog that damages and blinds.
- **ヘドリューム光線 Hedrium Ray** — "a crimson laser beam from its eye."
- **分裂・合体 division and recombination** — "a hive organism … can split apart into multiple smaller ones and recombine" → splits into flying saucer form and back.
- **エネルギー吸収 energy absorption.**

Weakness (canonical): "Hedorah relies on the moisture within the substance to function … vulnerable to being dried out by especially strong electrical currents … destroyed by the immense electrical currents generated by the Giant Electrodes." → **heat/electric dries him out**: Fire Ray 2×, plain atomic breath only 0.5× (you just spray him with more energy).

Silhouette brief: grey kelp-like sludge body; gold-and-black irises with red
sclera that blink sideways; humanoid Perfect Stage, quadrupedal Landing Stage,
saucer-shaped Flying Stage with a domed top.

Game read: **arena-denial boss**. The fight is about clearing his fog and
waiting for the split, not about raw trading. First fight where the natural
instinct (spam the beam) actively loses.

## Level 7 — キングギドラ King Ghidorah

| | |
|---|---|
| Debut | *Ghidorah, the Three-Headed Monster* (1964) |
| Title | 宇宙超怪獣 Space Super Monster / 宇宙の帝王 Emperor of the Cosmos |
| Species | three-headed space dragon |
| Height | 80 m (Showa→Heisei) |
| Element | 宇宙 cosmic |

Attacks (canonical):

- **重力光束 gravity beams** — "the golden lightning bolt-like Gravity Beams he spits from his three mouths … roughly equal in power to Godzilla's atomic breath … capable of causing large-scale explosions"; in *Rebirth of Mothra 3* he "could also combine all three gravity beams into a single devastating attack" → three-lane barrage that can merge into one super-laser.
- **飛行 flight** — "up to speeds of Mach 3."
- **ハリケーン暴風 hurricane winds** — "hurricane-force winds by flapping his wings, which are capable of blowing away buildings and knocking other kaiju off their feet."
- **首絞め constricting necks** — "constricted his necks around Godzilla in an attempt to strangle him, causing him to foam at the mouth" → grab.
- **火球 fireballs** — Cretaceous King Ghidorah "can spit Fireballs from his mouths."
- **バリア barriers** — "scales can manipulate the light from projectiles to form a protective barrier" → periodic beam reflect.

Silhouette brief: golden dragon, two legs, three long necks each with a horned
head, giant bat wings, two tails.

Game read: **the exam**. Ranged superiority on three lanes, a grab that
punishes standing still, a hurricane that punishes flying/jumping, and a
barrier that punishes beam spam. Everything the earlier nine levels taught is
tested at once.

## Level 8 — ビオランテ Biollante

| | |
|---|---|
| Debut | *Godzilla vs. Biollante* (1989), Heisei |
| Title | バイオ怪獣 Bio Monster / 巨大植物怪獣 Giant Plant Monster |
| Species | mutated rose / human / Godzilla hybrid |
| Height / weight | 85 m Flower Beast, 120 m Plant Beast / 60,000–200,000 t |
| Element | 植物 plant |

Attacks (canonical):

- **蔓藤 tendrils** — "can constrict and grapple with an enemy with her many tendrils and mouthed vines, each of which is capable of spitting a highly corrosive sap that can blind and burn an opponent" → multi-limb grab.
- **樹液 acidic sap** — "spit large quantities of radioactive corrosive sap from her gigantic maw … green electrical light before firing" → wide acid spray.
- **胞子 energy spores** — "can break apart her entire body into golden energy particles, ascend into the atmosphere, and return, healing completely" → she leaves the arena and comes back healed; also the transition between forms.
- **熱線 Heat Ray** — the Harvest variant "fires a heat ray from her secondary 'Godzilla' mouth" → used in phase 2.
- **荊棘 thorns / rose petals.**

Weakness (canonical): "the cellular division of Biollante's Flower Beast Form was abnormally affected by Godzilla's atomic breath … a direct shot … causing her core to bleed … a second blast caused her core to explode and her body to burst into flames." → **her glowing nucleus sac is a real weak point (2×) when exposed**, but Plant Beast form is "less" vulnerable. She also burns: Fire Ray 1.8×.

Silhouette brief: form one is a giant red rose with tiny jaws inside the flower,
long prehensile vines, hanging leaves, and a glowing yellowish-red nucleus sac
at the base. Form two has a mosasaur/crocodile head, a tongueless maw lined
with knife teeth, six large tusks (three per side), and a mass of vines.

Game read: **phase boss with a visible weak point**. Rooted phase one can't be
reached — you must hit the exposed sac. Phase two roams and thorns you for
meleeing her.

## Level 9 — スペースゴジラ SpaceGodzilla

| | |
|---|---|
| Debut | *Godzilla vs. SpaceGodzilla* (1994), Heisei |
| Title | 宇宙凶悪戦闘獣 Heinous Space Combat Beast / 破壊神 God of Destruction |
| Species | extraterrestrial crystalline Godzilla clone |
| Height / weight | 120 m / 80,000 t (combat), 720,000 t (flying mode) |
| Element | 結晶 crystal |

Attacks (canonical):

- **コロナビーム Corona Beam** — "an energy beam from his mouth **and shoulder crystals** … lightning-like … with a bluish-white curved tip … to stab and dig into armored targets before exploding" → fired from three emitters at once.
- **フォトン・リアクティブ・シールド Photon Reactive Shield** — "a crystal-like shield at will … to dissipate or even reflect energy beams, although he only used this ability to deflect Godzilla's atomic breath" → **hard counter to the player's best weapon**.
- **ホーミングゴースト Homing Ghost** — "raise crystals into the air and propel them at an opponent … Their quantity makes them hard to dodge, though they travel at a slow pace."
- **グラビ・トルネード Gravity Tornado** — "a form of telekinesis … allowing him to float or transport other kaiju through the air … Godzilla was completely unable to break free of his psychic grip" → throw.
- **地脈操作 geokinesis** — "control the ground around him by creating massive towering crystals" → ground spikes.
- **フォトン・ハリケーン Photon Hurricane** — "a ring-shaped electromagnetic wave … which disrupts electrical instruments" → disables the player's energy regen briefly.
- **飛行/浮遊 flight and levitation.**

Silhouette brief: navy-blue bulkier Godzilla with a smooth dark reddish-purple
abdomen, two enormous white shoulder crystals, white crystal dorsal fins, a
much longer tail ending in crystal spikes, and disproportionately short thin
arms. "has difficulty walking and he cannot run."

Game read: **the mirror that nulls your beam**. The shield forces melee, but
his short arms and slow walk make him easy to hit-and-run — until the Gravity
Tornado grabs you. The Photon Hurricane temporarily locks your energy, so
wasting it earlier in the fight is punished.

## Level 10 — デストロイア Destoroyah

| | |
|---|---|
| Debut | *Godzilla vs. Destoroyah* (1995), Heisei finale |
| Title | 完全生命体 Perfect Lifeform |
| Species | Oxygen Destroyer-mutated Precambrian crustacean superorganism |
| Height / weight | 40 m aggregate, 120 m perfect / 15,000–? t; wingspan 210 m |
| Forms | Micro, Crawl, Juvenile, Aggregate, Flying, Perfect |
| Element | 微氧 micro-oxygen |

Attacks (canonical):

- **マイクロオキシゲン Micro-Oxygen Beam / Oxygen Destroyer Ray** — "in all of his forms, Destoroyah's primary offensive weapon is his ability to expel Micro-Oxygen from his mouth … could penetrate metal and disintegrate organic matter."
- **ヴァリアブルスライサー Variable Slicer** — "the horn protruding from his forehead … generate a blade of energy powerful enough to cut through Godzilla's flesh … particularly effective in close-quarters combat" → close-range execute.
- **鋏・棘 pincers and spikes** — "Aggregate Form is equipped with spiked claws similar to those of a praying mantis, while his Flying and Perfect Forms possess a tail tipped with a grappling pincer that was strong enough to even hold Godzilla and pull him along. His tail also has the ability to drain energy."
- **分裂・合体 division and recombination** — "a composite lifeform formed from trillions of near-microscopic organisms, and as such he possesses vast capacities to adapt and regenerate" → splits into juveniles when badly hurt; they must be cleared before he recombines.
- **オキシジェン爆弾 Oxygen Destroyer / oxygen deprivation** — "Environmental corruption" → arena-wide hazard in the final phase.
- **エネルギー吸収 energy draining** (tail) → drains the player's radiation energy on grab.

Weakness (canonical): "Extreme temperatures can destroy the Micro-Oxygen in Destoroyah's body and seriously injure him. His Juvenile Forms were successfully killed using flamethrowers and cadmium shells, and Godzilla's red atomic ray severely injured Destoroyah's Perfect Form, forcing him to retreat twice." → **fire/heat is the answer: Fire Ray and the red ray are 2×** — which is exactly why the finale gives Godzilla the Burning form.

Silhouette brief: dark-red crustacean armour, multiple appendages, spiky
protrusions; Aggregate Form is a squat mantis-clawed crab, Perfect Form is a
tall horned devil-crab with a pincer-tipped tail and 210 m wings.

Game read: **everything, at once**. Swarm-split, arena poison, a close-range
cutter, and a grab that drains your energy — against a Godzilla who is
overheating and losing HP on a timer. Burning form and the Fire Ray unlock
exist so the player has the canonical answer in hand.

## Attribute / element matrix

Elements are deliberately coarse — the point is that the player must mix melee
and beam, not memorise a 9×9 table. Multipliers applied to the player's
damage, and vice versa:

| # | Kaiju | Element | Radiation | Melee | Fire | Signature counter taught |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Anguirus | 獣 beast | 0.6× | 1.0× | 1.0× | beam is not universal |
| 2 | Ebirah | 水 water | 1.4× | 0.8× | 1.0× | soft shell rewards beam |
| 3 | Rodan | 風 air | 1.5× airborne | 0.5× | 1.0× | anti-air timing |
| 4 | Mothra | 風 air | reflect | 1.3× | 1.0× | melee through the cloud |
| 5 | Gigan | 機械 mech | 1.3× | 0.7× | 1.0× | electronics vs plating |
| 6 | Hedorah | 毒 toxic | 0.5× | 1.2× | 2.0× | heat dries him out |
| 7 | King Ghidorah | 宇宙 cosmic | barrier reflect | 1.0× | 1.0× | all of the above at once |
| 8 | Biollante | 植物 plant | 2.0× on core | 0.8× thorns | 1.8× | aim the exposed nucleus |
| 9 | SpaceGodzilla | 結晶 crystal | shield reflect | 1.2× | 1.0× | forced melee |
| 10 | Destoroyah | 微氧 micro-oxygen | 1.0× (red ray 1.5×) | 1.0× | 2.0× | fire is canon |
